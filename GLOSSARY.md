# Medieval Portfolio

The single-scroll medieval-fantasy portfolio of Umrao Krishna Singh. One page, one scroll, read top to bottom.

## Structure

**Tapestry**:
The full page. One continuous scroll from hero to send seal.
_Avoid_: page, site, document

**Pull**:
One full-viewport section of the tapestry. Desktop has four pulls, mobile has seven.
_Avoid_: page, screen, slide

**Hero**:
The first pull. Eyebrow, name, title, two-line hook, two gates (View quests, Send a raven), plus image panel on desktop and ring sketch on mobile.
_Avoid_: header, banner, landing

**Quest**:
One project case-study pull. Exactly two exist: Quest I and Quest II. Title, quest text, role, runes, links, tale-so-far, sketch.
_Avoid_: project, case study, work sample

**Quest sketch**:
The ink-reveal sketch panel of a quest. Side-by-side with text on desktop, its own pull on mobile.
_Avoid_: screenshot, mockup, preview image

**About**:
The scribe's bio pull with the three ways of working. Side-by-side with Contact on desktop, its own pull on mobile.
_Avoid_: bio section (as a name), profile

**Contact**:
The raven-message pull. Email field plus message field only, closed by the send seal.
_Avoid_: form, contact form, footer

## Reading aids

**Progress**:
The roman-numeral counter at the foot of each pull (I OF IV on desktop, I OF VII on mobile).
_Avoid_: progress bar, page numbers, pagination

**Divider**:
The `-- ◆ --` ornament under hero and quest text.
_Avoid_: separator, rule, hr

**Colophon**:
The closing stamp line at the end of the tapestry.
_Avoid_: footer text, copyright line, sign-off

**Rune chip**:
One stack label in a quest's rune row (e.g. Three.js, GSAP).
_Avoid_: tag, badge, pill

**Tale-so-far**:
The two-line quest strip. Mood only, never outcomes or metrics.
_Avoid_: description, summary, results

## Interaction

**Kindle**:
The floating ember toggle that controls background music playback.
_Avoid_: audio button, mute button, speaker icon

**Arcane wax seal**:
The blood-orange wax seal binding the rolled tapestry on the scholar's desk, broken to begin the journey.
_Avoid_: start button, gate, enter button, intro button

**Seal crack**:
The brittle physical fracture sound produced when breaking the arcane wax seal.
_Avoid_: click sound, button audio, SFX

**Signet cursor**:
The brass signet hand with ring that serves as the desktop pointer, stamping down upon interaction.
_Avoid_: mouse pointer, custom cursor, hand icon

**Send seal**:
The wax-seal control at the foot of the tapestry, pressed to send the raven-message.
_Avoid_: submit button, send button, CTA, seal (unqualified)

**Ember reveal**:
Text arriving as a glowing fiery edge (~400ms) that cools into permanent ink.
_Avoid_: fade-in, typewriter, burn-in

**Ink reveal**:
Sketches and panels drawing themselves in like wet ink.
_Avoid_: lazy-load, sketch animation

**Charred reveal**:
The burn that brings any line or sketch in: a ragged charred edge with ember glow and heat shimmer consumes it, ink settles behind, sparks drift. Cools to ink over 400ms. The umbrella for ember reveal and ink reveal on desktop and mobile alike.
_Avoid_: gradient sweep, dissolve, burn effect

**Camera valley**:
The desk-view → read-framing → desk-view camera path, scrubbed by scroll position and fully reversible in both directions.
_Avoid_: zoom animation, outro, closing shot

**Desk view**:
The wide top-down shot of the whole desk — candles, rolled or unrolled parchment. Page top and page end.
_Avoid_: wide shot, zoomed-out view, landing view

**Read framing**:
The pushed-in view where the parchment fills the viewport and reading happens. The middle of the valley.
_Avoid_: zoomed-in view, reading mode, content view

**Desk zone**:
A thin free-scroll band at the very top or bottom of the page where snap is off, scroll scrubs the camera, and the 3D scene renders. Content pulls never begin inside one.
_Avoid_: overscroll area, dead zone, margin

**Frozen dwell**:
The resting state inside a desk zone where the stationary desk and candles hold while the flames continue to flicker live.
_Avoid_: paused canvas, freeze frame, idle mode


## Materials

**Parchment**:
The light writing surface of the tapestry and panels.
_Avoid_: background, card, beige

**Ink**:
The dark writing color. All text and line work.
_Avoid_: black, foreground, text color

**Ember**:
The orange accent. Reveals, pulses, the kindle glow. Never body text.
_Avoid_: orange, highlight, primary
