# Sound redesign: heavier thud, longer unroll, crackle synced to char, rebuilt drone

Status: accepted (2026-10-04).

All procedural (ADR-0001), gesture-gated and muted-first (ADR-0002) — unchanged. The current patches are retuned because they read thin: the thud drops deeper (140→30Hz triangle is too quick and bright; longer decay, real weight), the unroll runs longer and lower through a low-passed noise sweep, a crackle bed is synced to charred reveal passes, and the drone is rebuilt from layered detuned voices with slow filter movement and generated-impulse reverb instead of the two-oscillator hum. An optional candle-fire ambience layer joins the drone behind the kindle. Consequence: the sound engine grows a tiny graph (reverb impulse, crackle noise bed) but stays file-free; every new layer must remain kindle-gated, and crackle timing couples to the 400ms reveal token (ADR-0011).

## Update (2026-10-04, user iterate round 4: the drone is gone, the harp plays)

User verdict on the rebuilt drone: "just a random low frequency hum". The oscillator stack is removed entirely. In its place a generative mystic harp — Karplus-Strong plucked strings synthesized offline into cached buffers, a slow random-walk phrase generator in D dorian (accented phrase heads, cadence root under the last note, breathing gaps between phrases), dual detuned voices per pluck, everything through the shared generated-impulse reverb over a quiet fire hiss. Still 100% procedural and file-free; the CC0-track fallback (researched in docs/research/) is deliberately NOT shipped unless the user judges the harp insufficient after listening. Still gesture-gated and kindle-gated (ADR-0001/0002): the kindle button lights the harp; a kindle state restored from localStorage starts it on the first pointerdown. Consequence: the engine owns a per-note buffer cache (~10 notes) built once after the first gesture; the sound level of the site now depends on a phrase generator, which must never loop audibly.

## Update (2026-10-05, user iterate round 5: western-medieval organum)

The D minor PENTATONIC phrase walk read as East Asian (user: "very eastern/chinese sounding" — pentatonic gap-steps are the giveaway). The generator is rebuilt as WESTERN medieval: full diatonic D dorian over two octaves, phrase STRUCTURE instead of a wanderer (triad arpeggios, psalm-tone reciting figures with neighbor grace notes, stepwise arches), every phrase closing with a clausula — C -> C#4 (musica ficta) -> D with the counter dropping to the low final — or a soft cofinal close on A. Two-voice organum (user A5a): a parallel fourth or fifth below rides each phrase (the signature western color; harmonic and moving, unlike the rejected low rumble). Harp bus lowpass 3400->2900 Hz (gut-string dark). Still 100% procedural, kindle-gated, zero files (ADR-0001).

## Update (2026-10-05, user iterate round 5b: a composed tune)

User: "different tune and rhythm, not yet CC0." The dorian organum read aimless. The engine now plays a COMPOSED tune: G mixolydian (bright, folk-medieval), fixed A-A'-B-cadence form that repeats (it reads as a tune, not a wander), danced in 6/8 lilt pairs (long ~0.40s / short ~0.20s with jitter) instead of even eighths; the parallel fourth below (organum) rides every note and the close lands on the low final with the fifth under it. Harp bus lowpass 2900→3100. Still procedural, kindle-gated, zero files.
